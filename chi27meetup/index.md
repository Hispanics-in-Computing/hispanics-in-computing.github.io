**Meet-ups**: Latinos in Computing: Community, Mentorship, and Visibility


**Abstract:**
Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) work rooted in Latin America has grown steadily over the past two decades, drawing attention to how the region's languages, histories, and social realities shape the design and use of technology. Yet, Hispanic and Latin American researchers remain significantly underrepresented in the global Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) research ecosystem and within SIGCHI leadership. Building on CHI 2027's theme of "Bridges," this Meet-Up asks what it would take to build a sturdier bridge between Latin American HCI scholarship and the wider international community. We invite researchers, practitioners, students, and allies who work in, for, or about Latin America (or originally from there) to gather, compare notes, and connect at CHI 2027.
